Date + time — one instant, two fields

The datetime composite: a date leaf and a time leaf over ONE UTC instant. A date commit moves the day and keeps the wall clock (midnight when starting empty); a time commit sets the wall clock and keeps the day. Tab flows date → time. All three fields below bind the SAME required model — clear one and leave it: the error shows once the field is touched, the leaves' own rule. In the mat boxes the COMPOSITE is the form field's control (inlineMatFormField on it, never on a leaf): its leaves wear the bare chrome through it, keep their own error overlay closed, and the calendar drops below the box. A click on either leaf lands focus there.
